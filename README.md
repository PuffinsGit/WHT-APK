# WHT - Worked Hours Tracker

## Features

- **Work Hours Tracking** — Easily record and manage your worked hours
- **Calendar View** — Quickly see your recorded shifts and hours
- **Schedule** — Plan your expected working hours throughout the week
- **Today View** — Get a quick overview of today's work
- **Pay Tracking** — Keep track of estimated earnings from your hours
- **Export** — Export your work data for keeping records or further use
- **Settings** — Customise the app and manage its available options
- **Simple Interface** — Clean, easy-to-navigate layout designed around quick access
